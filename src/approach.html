Our approach

A clear starting point for your manpower requirement.

FROM ENQUIRY TO COORDINATION

How it works

  1. 01

    Share your requirement

    Choose the roles, quantities and hiring periods. Tell us the skills, experience and work location you need.

  2. 02

    Review the details

    Our team reviews your enquiry with you, clarifies the scope and discusses what can be arranged.

  3. 03

    Source and assess

    We source candidates and assess their fit, including in-person interviews at source. Employer interview coordination is subject to confirmation.

  4. 04

    Coordinate the next steps

    Once the requirement and candidates are agreed, we coordinate applicable documentation, visa and travel arrangements within the agreed scope.

Each engagement depends on the agreed requirement, candidate suitability and applicable procedures. Timelines and responsibilities are confirmed with you.

What we need from you

Share the roles, number of personnel, relevant skills, work location and intended hiring period. The minimum period is one year.

Frequently asked questions

Where do you currently source manpower?

We currently source manpower from India and Nepal. Additional sourcing locations may be considered according to employer requirements; availability and arrangements must be confirmed with our team.

How are candidates assessed?

Our team travels to the sourcing country to interview shortlisted candidates in person and assess them against the employer’s requirement. The skills and experience needed are agreed for each role.

Can we take part in candidate interviews?

You can request help coordinating in-person interviews when building your requirement. The format, location and arrangements are subject to confirmation and availability.

What is the minimum hiring period?

The minimum hiring period is one year. The enquiry builder supports periods from one to five years; speak to our team about requirements beyond that range.

Does sending an enquiry confirm an order?

No. An enquiry starts a discussion. Candidate suitability, availability, service scope and terms must be agreed before proceeding. There is no placement or deployment guarantee.

Who coordinates documentation, visas and travel?

We coordinate applicable visa and travel arrangements within the agreed engagement. Responsibilities, documentation and other requirements are confirmed before proceeding.

What support is included after placement?

Workforce support is agreed for each engagement. Please discuss the responsibilities and support you need with our team before proceeding.

How long does the process take?

Timing depends on the roles, personnel required, candidate availability and applicable procedures. Our team discusses the expected next steps after reviewing your requirement.

Can we request a role that is not listed?

Yes. Describe the role, duties and skills in your enquiry or contact us directly. Our team will confirm whether the requirement can be supported.
