Company profile

For employers in Sri Lanka

1 Victoria Foreign Employment (Pvt) Ltd helps employers in Sri Lanka source skilled trades and supporting personnel for construction, hospitality and household requirements.

  • Construction
  • Hospitality
  • Household support

Our work covers understanding the requirement, sourcing and assessing candidates, matching people to the work, and coordinating applicable visa and travel arrangements. Responsibilities and workforce support are agreed for each engagement.
